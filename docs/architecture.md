# Where the authority lives

Threshold locks the arrival record before a disagreement exists. Both parties accept the same tenancy terms and item inventory. Arrival photos become immutable commitments before deposit funding. Departure photos use the same item identifiers in separate participant-owned sets that can be corrected before the fixed recovery deadline. No application preview becomes a verdict.

The registry owns parties, condition clauses, item definitions, evidence commitments and network timestamps. Its agreement hash covers the full terms and immutable reference snapshots. The agreed departure date anchors notice and depreciation; a mutual earlier date cannot be backdated or extend the tenancy. A contract timestamp proves when a commitment was submitted. A camera timestamp does not prove when a photo was taken. Both parties must accept the arrival record. Replacement prices come from published references attached to each item, bounded by a jointly accepted replacement ceiling. HUD Appendix 5D supplies useful life as a neutral depreciation reference. It is a sample schedule, not a universal legal mandate.

The court fetches committed bytes, verifies SHA256, and independently evaluates each pair on every validator. Classification, deduction band, item identity, expenditure support, bad faith and new damage count match exactly. Reasons may differ. Dates and amounts are evaluated in a deterministic sandbox. Missing, late or empty claims resolve deterministically without external evidence. Claimed ambiguity becomes UNDETERMINED and can be retried with corrected evidence. Inconclusive rehearings preserve decided originals; either party can complete a bonded review before its three-day deadline. Bond expiry and the unconditional departure-day-36 recovery path bound settlement even when participants or evidence disappear. Failed network consensus cannot fabricate a verdict.

The escrow receives actual native value. A recorded amount never substitutes for funding. StudioNet native balances are simulated by the network, and the interface identifies them as Studio credits. This deployment does not hold fiat money. Escrow reserves are separate from the tenant deposit. Reserve funding requires a funded deposit and accepted arrival. Available reserve pays penalties; unpaid penalties become separate debt without blocking principal release or spending another tenancy's deposit. Reserve top-ups remain possible after departure. Wallet payouts use EVM external value transfers after finalization. A punitive finding requires an upheld tenant appeal and bad faith, not simply a missed deadline.

Privy owns email authentication and embedded account access. The app hides wallet mechanics and submits authorized actions with the user's account. The deployment key never becomes a shared tenant signing key. The backend verifies Privy sessions, checks tenancy membership, persists a non authoritative Neon index, and serves immutable evidence objects. Unsigned preparations expire and cannot lock either participant’s other actions. Accepted execution is distinct from finalized execution; contract reads use final state. Payout completion requires exact, finalized, credited external transfers linked to the settlement receipt. Either participant can resume receipt reconciliation.

Original images never go on chain. Evidence URLs are capability links: validators need access to the bytes, so these links and the photos are not private to chain observers. The capture flow requires people and documents to be excluded, allows retakes and strips metadata before commitment. Users review the final image before upload. A local MediaPipe detector checks for faces before upload. Detection can miss faces; documents and screens require explicit human review.

The first legal profile is ordinary California residential tenancy governed by Civil Code 1950.5, without the small landlord deposit exception. Local exceptions and other rental classes are not silently mapped to California. The law reference, profile scope and supported checks are visible. Statutory checks include the deposit ceiling, a recorded itemised notice within 21 calendar days, and required evidence. The network record proves notice registration, not postal delivery. Both parties agree that the app record serves as their itemised condition notice. Deductions are bounded by snapshotted actual-expenditure evidence, claims, depreciation and agreed bands. Complex disputes can be reviewed within fixed deadlines; unavailable sources cannot hold escrow indefinitely.
